Our approach

What we're building toward

Our work is guided by three outcomes for the properties we serve, delivered with local support.

  • More direct bookings. Tools that help properties take bookings through their own channels.
  • Simpler operations. Fewer manual steps at the front desk and in housekeeping.
  • Better guest experience. Faster arrivals and easier communication before, during and after a stay.
  • Guest payments stay with the property. Our booking software does not collect or hold guest funds. Payments are processed by licensed third-party payment providers and settled directly to the hotel or operator.
  • Personal data handled responsibly. We are committed to meeting our obligations under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.
