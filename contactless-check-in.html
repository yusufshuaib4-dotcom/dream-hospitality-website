Before you plan

A few useful answers.

Will it work with our existing door locks?

Digital key integration is planned, but supported hardware, vendors and compatibility have not been confirmed. Those details need to be checked before making any purchasing decision.

Is this product available now?

No. This product is in development. First modules in the suite are planned for 2027; individual module dates and final capabilities will be confirmed later.

What will it cost?

The software is planned mainly as a subscription, with implementation, hotel group licensing and support options. Pricing and packages have not been announced.

How can I prepare?

Use our property brief tool to note your property type, size and the areas you would like to improve. It creates a brief on your device without sending it.
