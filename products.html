How it will be offered

Subscription software with local support

Our software will be offered mainly on a subscription (SaaS) basis, with implementation, group licensing and support options around it.

  • Subscriptions. The main way properties will use our software.
  • Implementation. Set-up and onboarding for each property.
  • Hotel group licences. Options for groups running several properties.
  • Support retainers. Ongoing support, based in Kuala Lumpur.
  • Guest payments stay with the property. Our booking software does not collect or hold guest funds. Payments are processed by licensed third-party payment providers and settled directly to the hotel or operator.
