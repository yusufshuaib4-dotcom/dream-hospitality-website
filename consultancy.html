IT consultancy and support

Technology advice and support for hospitality businesses

Choosing, setting up and running hotel systems takes time most operators don't have. We help hospitality businesses plan, implement and run the technology behind their operations.

Technology assessments

A clear review of the systems you use today, where they fall short and what to prioritise.

  • Review of current systems and workflows
  • Gaps, risks and priorities
  • Practical recommendations

System selection

Help comparing and choosing booking, property management and guest-facing tools that fit your property and budget.

  • Requirements for your property
  • Comparison of suitable options
  • Support with vendor decisions

Implementation

Planning and delivery when you introduce new hospitality systems.

  • Project planning and set-up
  • Data migration
  • Staff onboarding

Ongoing IT support

Locally based support to keep day-to-day systems running.

  • Support for hospitality systems
  • Troubleshooting and maintenance
  • Support retainers
