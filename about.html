About us

Rooted in Malaysia. Built for hospitality.

Dream Hospitality Sdn. Bhd. is a private company incorporated in Malaysia in 2026 and based in Kuala Lumpur. We develop software for the hospitality industry and provide IT consultancy and support to hospitality businesses.

✦
Vision

To be a trusted provider of practical, affordable hospitality technology in Malaysia and Southeast Asia.

Mission

To help hotels, resorts and vacation rental operators increase direct bookings, simplify operations and improve guest experience through reliable, locally supported software.

Our market

Starting in Malaysia, growing across Southeast Asia

We focus first on hotels, resorts and rental operators in Malaysia, particularly independent and boutique properties, vacation rental and serviced apartment operators, and small and mid-sized hotel groups.

As we grow, we plan to serve hospitality businesses in Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam and the Philippines.

Our commitments
  • Guest payments stay with the property. Our booking software does not collect or hold guest funds; payments are processed by licensed third-party providers and settled directly to the hotel or operator.
  • Personal data handled responsibly. We are committed to meeting our obligations under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.
